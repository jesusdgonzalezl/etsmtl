## Space systems engineering

We investigate mission-driven spacecraft architectures, small-satellite design, space qualification, and the interactions between hardware, software, and the space environment.

## Spacecraft power and energy

Our work spans photovoltaic generation, power conversion and distribution, energy storage, power budgets, and reliable electrical power systems for resource-constrained spacecraft.

## Intelligent and autonomous systems

We apply artificial intelligence, machine learning, modeling, and control to improve the autonomy and resilience of space missions.

## Cyber-physical systems

We connect physical spacecraft behavior with sensing, computation, communication, and control to study complete systems rather than isolated components.

## Prospective graduate students

We welcome inquiries from motivated master's and doctoral candidates whose interests align with our research program.

When contacting the group, please include:

- A concise description of your research interests
- Your curriculum vitae
- Academic transcripts
- Relevant publications, projects, or technical portfolio
- Your intended program and preferred start date

## Undergraduate research

Research projects may be available for ÉTS undergraduate students. Opportunities depend on current projects, funding, and supervision capacity.
